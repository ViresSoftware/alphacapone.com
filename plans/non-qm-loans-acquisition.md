## 1. Service verticals (full content)

### Non-QM Loan Acquisitions

**Private Access to Non-QM Portfolio Liquidity**

Alpha Capital One acquires performing residential Non-QM loan portfolios for qualified sellers seeking a credible counterparty, disciplined review, and efficient execution.

**Portfolio Acquisitions**  
Direct acquisition of performing residential Non-QM loan pools.

**Loan Tape Analysis**  
Review of collateral profile, borrower characteristics, concentration, and performance.

**Structured Transactions**  
Clear coordination from initial review through diligence, documentation, and closing.

**Seller Liquidity**  
Execution for lenders, originators, and portfolio holders seeking an efficient sale process.

**How We Operate**

1. **Introduction & Scope** — Assess portfolio size, composition, and transaction fit.
2. **Underwriting & Analysis** — Review tape, collateral, and performance characteristics.
3. **Structure & Documentation** — Align on pricing, diligence scope, and transaction terms.
4. **Execution & Settlement** — Coordinate closing, transfer mechanics, and settlement.

**Closing line**  
Confidential review for performing Non-QM opportunities presented by qualified sellers.
